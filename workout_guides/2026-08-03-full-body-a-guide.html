Full-Body A

Tuesday · Chest & Back Emphasis · Life Time Battery Park · ~45–60 min
Before you start — warm-up (5–10 min): 5 min light cardio (brisk walk, bike, or rower, easy pace) → arm circles and band pull-aparts, 15–20 reps each → one light warm-up set of exercise #1 (light plates, 12–15 easy reps) before loading real weight.

Several moves here have you lying, seated, or bending forward: move between positions calmly rather than popping up fast — Nifedipine can cause dizziness on standing. Hydrate before, during, and after.
Today's Full-Body Focus
Every full-body session hits a push, a pull, and a leg pattern, then finishes with shoulder isolation, a pec/back accessory move, and core. Today's push and pull lean toward pecs and back width; legs get the quad-dominant leg press. Lateral raises are in the isolation slot twice a week — it's the single biggest lever for the “3D” shoulder look, so it shows up here and again Saturday.
