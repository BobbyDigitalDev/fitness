Full-Body B

Thursday · Shoulders & Posterior Chain Emphasis · Life Time Battery Park · ~45–60 min
Before anything else: start your Fitbit workout tracking now, before you do your warm-up — it's easy to forget once you're moving, and starting late throws off the session's cardio load, zone minutes, and calorie numbers.

Warm-up (5–10 min): 5 min light cardio (brisk walk, bike, or rower, easy pace) → arm circles and band pull-aparts, 15–20 reps each → one light warm-up set of exercise #1 (light plates, 12–15 easy reps) before loading real weight.

Several moves here have you lying, seated, or bending forward: move between positions calmly rather than popping up fast — Nifedipine can cause dizziness on standing. Hydrate before, during, and after.
Today's Full-Body Focus
Today's push is overhead instead of flat/incline — it hits front and side delt directly. The pull moves to horizontal rowing for back thickness, and the leg pattern flips to hamstring/glute-dominant with the Romanian deadlift. Isolation work targets the rear delt — the head that finishes the “3D” shoulder look from behind.

6. Half-Kneeling DB Anti-Rotation Press
CORE · ANTI-ROTATION
Deep core/obliques — resisting a twisting force.
Half-kneeling stance (one knee down, opposite foot planted forward), holding a dumbbell at your chest with both hands. Press it straight out until your arms are extended, resisting the urge to let your torso rotate toward the working side, then return to your chest under control. Complete all reps on one side before switching — keep your hips and shoulders square the whole time. Rest 45-60 sec before your next set.
