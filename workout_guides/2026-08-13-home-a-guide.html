Chest & Back Emphasis · Home — 2x15lb Dumbbells · ~35–45 min
Before you start — warm-up (5–10 min): 5 min light cardio in place (marching, jumping jacks, or a brisk walk if you've got room) → arm circles, 15–20 reps each direction → one light warm-up set of exercise #1 with an easy pace before your working sets.

Several moves here have you lying, kneeling, or bending forward: move between positions calmly rather than popping up fast — Nifedipine can cause dizziness on standing. Hydrate before, during, and after.

Why the reps run higher than your gym guides: two fixed 15lb dumbbells can't progress by adding weight the way the gym's stack does. Progression here comes from reps (working toward the top of each range, then adding a 4th set once you're there), slower tempo (2-3 sec lowering phase), and short rest (60-75 sec between sets) instead. Back to the regular Life Time program whenever you're back at the gym.
Today's Full-Body Focus
Same push/pull/leg/isolation/accessory/core structure as the Life Time guides, rebuilt for two fixed 15lb dumbbells and floor space only. Push and pull both go flat on the floor since there's no bench; the leg slot is a goblet squat; push-ups fill the second chest accessory slot since 15lb alone won't tax pecs enough at this point. Rep ranges run higher (15-20) than your gym guides — see the note above on why.

The Session
1. Dumbbell Floor Press
PUSH · CHEST
Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor. Hold a dumbbell in each hand just outside your chest, elbows bent under the dumbbells. Press straight up until your arms are extended, then lower with control until your upper arms touch the floor — let the floor stop you rather than dropping into it. Exhale on the press, inhale on the way down; keep the dumbbells stacked over your chest, not drifting toward your face. Rest 60-75 sec before your next set.

2. Bent-Over Two-Arm Row
PULL · BACK
Stand feet hip-width apart, hinge forward at the hips to about 45° with a flat back and soft knees, dumbbells hanging straight down. Row both dumbbells to your ribs by driving your elbows back and up, squeezing your shoulder blades together at the top, then lower under control to a full hang. If your lower back starts to round, stand a little taller in the hinge — don't use body momentum to heave the weight. Rest 60-75 sec before your next set.

3. Goblet Squat
LEG · QUAD/GLUTE
Hold one dumbbell vertically at your chest, both hands cupping the top end, feet just outside shoulder-width with toes turned slightly out. Sit your hips back and down, chest up, dumbbell close to your body, going to at least parallel if comfortable. Drive through your whole foot to stand, and keep your knees tracking over your toes rather than caving in. Rest 60-75 sec before your next set.

4. Dumbbell Lateral Raise
SHOULDER ISOLATION · SIDE DELT
Stand tall, a dumbbell in each hand at your sides with a slight bend in the elbows. Raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height, leading with your elbows rather than your hands, then lower slowly. No swinging or using your torso to heave the weight — 15lb is enough to feel this properly if you keep it strict. Rest 45-60 sec before your next set.

5. Push-Up
PEC ACCESSORY · TRICEP
Hands on the floor slightly wider than your shoulders, straight line from head to heels, core braced. Lower your chest toward the floor with elbows at roughly 45° to your torso, then press back to full extension. If your hips start to sag or pike before the set's done, drop to your knees for the rest of the reps rather than losing the straight line. Rest 60-75 sec before your next set.

6. Forearm Plank
CORE
Forearms on the floor with elbows under your shoulders, legs extended behind you, balanced on your toes. Hold a straight line from shoulders to hips to ankles by bracing your core and squeezing your glutes — no sagging hips, no piked-up butt. Breathe steadily throughout instead of holding your breath. Rest 30-45 sec between sets.
